# Overview

The AI Engine is Vera's centralized intelligence platform responsible for integrating, orchestrating, securing, and managing Artificial Intelligence capabilities across the entire ecosystem.

Rather than embedding Large Language Model (LLM) calls directly into individual applications, every AI interaction passes through the AI Engine. This creates a single platform for model routing, prompt orchestration, context management, safety enforcement, cost optimization, observability, and provider abstraction.

The AI Engine allows every Vera service to consume intelligence as an internal platform capability instead of treating AI as an external dependency.

Examples include

* AI Assistants
* Customer Support
* Intelligent Search
* Document Analysis
* Code Generation
* Workflow Planning
* Data Extraction
* AI Agents
* Report Generation
* Enterprise Knowledge Retrieval

The AI Engine serves as the Intelligence Layer of Vera.

# Architecture Decision Record (ADR-024)

**Decision:** Vera centralizes all AI capabilities behind a provider-agnostic AI Engine rather than allowing applications to integrate directly with individual model providers.

**Context:** Direct provider integrations create vendor lock-in, inconsistent safety controls, duplicated prompt management, fragmented observability, and higher operational complexity.

**Decision Outcome:**

* AI providers are abstracted behind adapters.
* Prompt orchestration is centralized.
* Context, safety, and governance are applied consistently.
* Applications consume a stable AI platform API regardless of the underlying model provider.

**Consequences:**

* Providers can be added or replaced without changing application code.
* AI governance becomes consistent across the platform.
* Costs, latency, and quality can be optimized centrally.
* Future multimodal and autonomous AI capabilities integrate naturally into the platform architecture.

# Design Goals

The AI Engine serves as Vera's centralized intelligence platform, providing secure, scalable, provider-independent access to artificial intelligence capabilities across every product and service.

Its architecture emphasizes model abstraction, context-aware orchestration, enterprise governance, observability, cost optimization, and seamless integration with the AI Agent Engine, Memory Engine, Knowledge Engine, Vector Engine, Workflow Engine, Automation Engine, and the broader Vera intelligence ecosystem.
