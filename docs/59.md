# Overview

The Fraud Detection Engine is responsible for identifying, preventing, investigating, and responding to fraudulent activities across the Vera platform.

While the **Threat Detection Engine** focuses on identifying malicious attacks against the platform, the Fraud Detection Engine focuses on identifying **financial abuse, account misuse, identity fraud, service abuse, marketplace fraud, billing manipulation, and transactional anomalies**.

The Fraud Detection Engine combines behavioral analytics, risk intelligence, transaction analysis, identity verification, device trust, machine learning (future), and policy evaluation to determine whether an action is legitimate or fraudulent.

It operates continuously and provides real-time fraud prevention without introducing unnecessary friction for legitimate users.

# Core Principles

## Fraud Is Behavioral

Fraud rarely depends on a single event.

Detection relies on patterns, relationships, history, and behavioral consistency.

---

## Real-Time Prevention

Fraud should be detected before irreversible actions occur.

Examples include

* Payments
* Withdrawals
* Account recovery
* Organization transfers
* Marketplace purchases

---

## Context Matters

Fraud analysis combines

* Identity
* Device
* Session
* Risk score
* Transaction history
* Behavioral history
* Organization policies

---

## Low Friction

Legitimate users should experience minimal interruptions.

Additional verification should occur only when justified.

---

## Explainable Decisions

Every fraud decision must provide

* Detection reason
* Evidence
* Confidence
* Recommended action
* Supporting signals

# Architecture Decision Record (ADR-019)

**Decision:** Vera evaluates all high-value identity, financial, and transactional operations through a centralized Fraud Detection Engine before execution.

**Reason:** Fraud often spans multiple identities, devices, payment methods, and behavioral patterns. A centralized engine enables consistent detection, faster response, reduced financial loss, and improved customer trust.

**Consequences:**

* Fraud detection becomes consistent across all Vera services.
* Marketplace, billing, wallet, and payment systems share a unified fraud model.
* Automated responses reduce fraud exposure in real time.
* Future AI-based fraud models can be introduced without redesigning business services.

---

# Design Goals

The Fraud Detection Engine provides Vera's intelligent fraud prevention infrastructure by continuously analyzing transactions, identities, devices, payment activity, and behavioral patterns to identify fraudulent activity before it causes harm.

Its architecture emphasizes real-time analysis, explainable decisions, scalable behavioral intelligence, enterprise-grade investigation workflows, and seamless integration with the Risk Engine, Threat Detection Engine, Payment Engine, Marketplace Engine, Billing Engine, and the broader Vera security ecosystem.
