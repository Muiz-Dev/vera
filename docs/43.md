# Overview

The Organization Engine manages companies, startups, teams, agencies, educational institutions, and any other collaborative entity within the Vera platform.

While the **User Engine** represents an individual's identity and the **Developer Engine** represents an individual's developer workspace, the **Organization Engine** represents a shared workspace where multiple developers collaborate on applications, infrastructure, API keys, billing, deployments, and resources.

Organizations are first-class citizens within Vera.

Every resource can belong either to an individual Developer or to an Organization.

---

# Vision

Organizations should become the collaborative foundation of Vera.

Whether a single startup with two developers or a global enterprise with thousands of engineers, the Organization Engine should provide a secure and scalable collaboration model.

# Core Principles

## Organization is an Entity

Organizations are independent platform entities.

They are not extensions of user accounts.

---

## Multiple Members

One organization may contain many developers.

```text id="org01"
Organization

↓

Developers

↓

Applications

↓

Resources
```

---

## Multiple Organizations

A developer may belong to multiple organizations simultaneously.

---

## Ownership Separation

Applications, API Keys, Billing Accounts, Storage, and Deployments may belong to an Organization instead of an individual Developer.

---

## Extensible

Organizations should support future enterprise features without redesign.

---

# Responsibilities

The Organization Engine is responsible for

* Organization creation
* Organization updates
* Organization deletion
* Membership management
* Invitations
* Ownership transfer
* Organization settings
* Organization metadata
* Organization lifecycle

The Organization Engine is NOT responsible for

* Authentication
* Authorization policy evaluation
* Billing calculations
* Application deployment

# Architecture Decision Record (ADR-003)

**Decision:** Organizations are independent ownership entities separate from Users and Developers.

**Reason:** Separating Organizations from individual identities enables collaborative development, shared ownership, enterprise scalability, and resource continuity even when members join or leave.

**Consequences:**

* Resources can belong to Organizations instead of individuals.
* Developers may participate in multiple Organizations.
* Ownership survives individual personnel changes.
* Enterprise features can be added without redesigning the ownership model.

---

# Design Goals

The Organization Engine provides the collaborative foundation of Vera by enabling secure, scalable, and flexible shared workspaces.

It ensures that applications, infrastructure, and platform resources can be managed collectively while maintaining clear ownership, strong security, complete auditability, and seamless collaboration for teams ranging from small startups to global enterprises.
