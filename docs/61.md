# Overview

The Workflow Engine is Vera's orchestration platform responsible for executing, coordinating, monitoring, and recovering long-running business processes across every engine within the platform.

Unlike the Policy Engine, which determines **what should happen**, or the Rules Engine, which evaluates **business logic**, the Workflow Engine determines **how a process is executed from start to finish**.

A workflow represents an ordered collection of tasks, decisions, events, conditions, timers, retries, compensating actions, and approvals that together achieve a business objective.

Examples include:

* User Registration
* Organization Provisioning
* Password Recovery
* Subscription Upgrade
* Marketplace Checkout
* Application Deployment
* Identity Verification
* Incident Response
* Infrastructure Provisioning
* AI Agent Execution

The Workflow Engine is designed as a durable orchestration system capable of executing workflows that may last from milliseconds to several months while surviving crashes, deployments, regional failures, and infrastructure restarts.

Failure handling becomes inconsistent.

Retries become unreliable.

Observability disappears.

```text

↓

↓

↓

↓

↓

```

# Architecture Decision Record (ADR-021)

**Decision:** Vera adopts a durable workflow orchestration engine instead of embedding long-running business processes inside individual services.

**Context:** Modern distributed systems require coordination across multiple services, retries, external APIs, human approvals, and asynchronous events. Embedding orchestration into application code leads to tight coupling, inconsistent error handling, and poor observability.

**Decision Outcome:**

* All complex business processes are modeled as workflows.
* Workflow state is persisted independently from execution workers.
* Workers remain stateless and horizontally scalable.
* Recovery begins from the last durable checkpoint rather than restarting the entire process.

**Consequences:**

* Business logic becomes reusable and observable.
* Distributed failures become recoverable.
* Long-running operations survive deployments and outages.
* Platform-wide orchestration remains consistent across every Vera engine.

# Design Goals

The Workflow Engine serves as Vera's orchestration backbone, enabling durable, observable, and fault-tolerant execution of business processes across the entire platform.

Its architecture emphasizes resilience, distributed coordination, scalability, recoverability, and operational transparency while integrating seamlessly with the Event Engine, Queue Engine, Scheduler Engine, Rules Engine, Policy Engine, Automation Engine, AI Engine, and every platform service that executes long-running or multi-step operations.
