# Overview

The Knowledge Engine is Vera's centralized enterprise knowledge platform responsible for ingesting, organizing, validating, governing, indexing, and serving trusted organizational knowledge to applications, AI systems, autonomous agents, and users.

Unlike the **AI Memory Engine**, which stores experiences, or the **AI Context Engine**, which assembles temporary context, the Knowledge Engine stores **authoritative information** that represents the official source of truth for an organization.

Knowledge may originate from

* Internal documentation
* Policies
* Standard operating procedures
* Technical specifications
* Product manuals
* Wikis
* Contracts
* Knowledge bases
* APIs
* External data sources

Every knowledge asset is versioned, classified, searchable, and governed throughout its lifecycle.

# Design Philosophy

Information becomes knowledge only after it is validated.

The Knowledge Engine stores trusted organizational knowledge—not temporary conversations, generated content, or personal memories.

Every knowledge object should have a known owner, version, confidence level, and governance policy.

---

The Knowledge Engine is responsible for

* Knowledge ingestion

# Architecture Decision Record (ADR-028)

**Decision:** Vera centralizes organizational knowledge into a dedicated Knowledge Engine rather than distributing documentation across individual application services.

**Context:** Enterprise knowledge is frequently consumed by humans, AI systems, workflows, and search services. A centralized repository improves governance, discoverability, version control, and consistency.

**Decision Outcome:**

* Knowledge is validated before publication.
* Published versions are immutable.
* Knowledge is indexed for semantic retrieval.
* Ownership and governance are mandatory.

**Consequences:**

* AI systems receive authoritative information instead of unverified content.
* Documentation becomes reusable across the platform.
* Compliance and audit requirements are easier to satisfy.
* Enterprise knowledge evolves independently from application releases.

# Design Goals

The Knowledge Engine serves as Vera's authoritative enterprise knowledge platform, transforming documents, policies, procedures, and technical information into governed, searchable, AI-ready knowledge assets.

Its architecture emphasizes validation, version control, semantic indexing, governance, scalability, and seamless integration with the AI Engine, AI Agent Engine, AI Context Engine, Vector Engine, Search Engine, and the broader Vera intelligence ecosystem.
